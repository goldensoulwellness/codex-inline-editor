PRIVACY

Your edits stay with you.

Effective September 26, 2026. This policy describes Codex In-Line Text Editor and its optional local companion.

What the extension processes

To edit and identify text, the extension processes the current webpage URL, the text sections you edit, CSS selectors identifying those sections, and your replacement text. It adds a toolbar to compatible pages only when activated, unless you explicitly enable automatic loading. It does not request browser history, cookies, passwords, microphone, camera, or account credentials.

Local storage

Unsent edits and per-tab connection details are held in extension-only browser session storage. Connection details include a source directory, chat identifier, local endpoint, and a random authorization token. Draft storage is limited to five pages per tab and 60 KB per page. Closing the tab or restarting the browser clears this session data. The automatic-pencil preference is stored locally until changed or the extension is removed. The extension does not put drafts into a website's local or session storage.

Copy and Send

Copy writes replacement instructions to your clipboard only when you click Copy. Other applications or clipboard history tools may retain clipboard contents. When you click Send in a paired tab, the extension sends the edit batch to a program on your own computer over a random authenticated loopback endpoint. The companion passes the page URL, source directory, selectors, and before/after text to your installed Codex CLI for the exact connected chat. Codex and its service provider handle the resulting message under your account settings and their own privacy terms. Do not submit sensitive text unless you intend to share it with that chat.

No publisher collection

The extension and companion contain no analytics, advertising, publisher telemetry, remote code, or publisher data-collection server. The publisher does not receive or sell your browsing data or edits. The extension does not send webpage contents to AI services directly or train models. Once a message is sent through Codex, retention and processing are controlled by that service, not this extension.

Webpage behavior

Editing changes the live page DOM. The website's own scripts may observe those DOM changes and remain governed by that website's policies. The extension is not a sandbox for website scripts. Only edit pages you trust. A URL may contain private query values; review your copied or sent instructions accordingly.

Permissions and control

Website access enables the toolbar button to activate text editing on compatible webpages and supports optional automatic loading. Storage saves settings, drafts, and explicit tab connections. Active-tab access lets the popup operate on the tab you selected. Loopback host access connects to the optional companion. You can turn off automatic loading, limit site access, clear drafts with ×, disconnect by closing the tab or browser, and uninstall at any time. Discarding a draft does not recall an already sent chat message.

Limited Use

Any use or transfer of user data by Codex In-Line Text Editor is limited to its single purpose of in-place text editing and user-requested delivery of those edits, and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Use the setup and troubleshooting page (do not include private edits or credentials), or the developer contact shown on the Chrome Web Store listing. Updates to this policy will be published with the extension and on its publisher website.
